## Scope And Product Contract

- Intended outcome: explain the Corvi offer to custom marble shops, estimate possible operational losses before collecting contact data, and qualify requests for a free initial evaluation.
- Personas and tenants: owners or managers of small and medium custom marble shops; public, non-tenant route.
- Affected apps/modules: `apps/site` marmorarias route, estimator and lead form; `apps/api` public lead intake.
- Adjacent regressions: existing public lead contract and Commercial default email requirement.
- Trust/data boundaries: no calculator answers or lead PII in analytics; no fabricated email; no shared remote database mutation during QA.
- Out of scope: paid Raio-X delivery, CRM follow-up and production deployment.
