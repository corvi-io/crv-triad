## Scope And Product Contract

- Intended outcome: administrators choose ordered research themes and every completed lead reading derives its summary and sections from that snapshot.
- Personas: synthetic administrator and active member; one workspace (multi-tenancy does not yet exist).
- Affected apps/modules: authenticated web and Commercial API/persistence/Trigger worker.
- Trust boundaries: system-owned prompts, public sourced professional data, administrator-only mutation, immutable run snapshots.
- Out of scope: live paid/provider research quality, deployment, shared data, and multi-tenant isolation.
